# Contributor License Agreement

> **Draft, not in force.** This page is a placeholder that records the intent. The actual agreement must be drafted and reviewed by a lawyer before anyone is asked to sign it. Do not treat the text below as legal advice or as a final agreement.

## Why a CLA

The core of this project is licensed under AGPL-3.0, and the maintainers also plan to offer a hosted version and commercial add-ons (the `ee/` folder). To do both, the project needs the right to distribute contributions under the AGPL and under a commercial license.

## Intended terms (to be confirmed)

- You keep the copyright to your contribution.
- You grant the project owner a broad licence to use, modify and distribute your contribution, including under licences other than the AGPL.
- You confirm that the contribution is your own work, or that you have the right to submit it.
- You confirm that your employer, if it has rights to your work, allows the contribution.
